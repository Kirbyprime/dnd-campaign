## Description

A magical, tattoo. Its appearance is that of unknown rune seemingly carved into the skin. The flesh where the tattoo resides appears raw but does not bleed nor does it cause any physical discomfort. Any attempts to cover it or reproduce it causes the victim to convulse in unimaginable pain.

Once attached to a person, it can no longer be moved. If the wielder is dead, it will detach itself and fly towards the nearest living being and etch itself onto their skin.

## Additional Details

The wielder gains immunity to Madness.
