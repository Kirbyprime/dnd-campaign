#### Description
Sable sits slumped on the floor, his robes in tatters. Some sort of tattoo visible on the side of forearm, partially covered by his robes. There is evidence of torture all throughout his body. He eyes your group  with rage in his eyes, barely containing his urge to jump out and strangle each and every one of you. In his left hand you see a wooden fork that he fidgets with constantly while in his right, you see a makeshift dagger made out of animal bones. You hear him grunt at you angrily but no words come out. As you look at his mouth, you see signs of dried blood and emptiness where his tongue would have been.
