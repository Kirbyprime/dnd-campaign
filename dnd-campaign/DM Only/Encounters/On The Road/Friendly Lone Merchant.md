## Setup
* A lone merchant appears from beyond the horizon heading towards you.
	* He's riding on a wooden wagon pulled by a pony. 
	* The merchant is just traveling past the party along the road stopping on any village they come across.
	* He stops by and offers some food after noticing that your party seems famished
	* Unable to give them a ride since the wagon is full.
* The pony pleads with the party to kill the merchant and set him free.
	* Mentions that the merchant whips the pony. (Merchant says that's normal training)
	* Offers to carry them to their next destination if they do so.

## Enemies
* Commoner

## Tactics
The merchant is just traveling past the party along the road stopping on any village they come across. The merchant continuously whips the pony.

## Loot
- 3lb Spices (Mustard, Oregano, Maple Syrup) worth 70g

## Expected Outcomes
* Party ignores it
* Party kills the merchant.
	* At their destination, the pony will refuse to travel further and requests to be set free.
		* If the party frees it, the pony thanks them and runs away.
		* If party does not free it, the pony refuses to move and will eventually starve.
		* [[Loraxia Thornweaver]] will get a standalone option from the Whispers to send the pony's soul to oblivion, leaving the party with an obedient and mindless pony to carry them.
