## Setup
2 hooded monks walk past the party. As the party passes, one of the monks mentions to the other that one in the party appears to be Qing be betrayer. The other monk is shocked and "psi-jumps" in front of the party and confronts Qing the 2nd telling him to return the honoured Tressym (that went missing when Qing left) that he stole when he left the Creche. 

## Enemies
- 2 Githzerai Monks

## Tactics
One of the monks will challenge Qing to a duel then attack immediately (only those 2 roll initiative). If any of the others interfere in any way, both Gith will attempt to run away vowing vengeance on Qing.

## Loot
Some nuts and loaves of bread.

## Expected Outcomes
- Qing defeats the monk and can choose to either let them live or kill them
	- live: the monks leave but promise that others will come and find him until the Tressym is returned
	- die: the other monk runs and swears vengeance upon Qing
- Qing is defeated. The monk spits on him and leaves, mentioning that someone so weak couldn't have taken the honoured Tressym.
- The party intervenes and kills both monks.
- The party intervenes and one or both monks escape.
