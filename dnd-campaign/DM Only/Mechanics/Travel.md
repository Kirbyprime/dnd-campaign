## Walking

| Pace   | Per Minute | Per Hour | Per Day  |
| ------ | ---------- | -------- | -------- |
| Fast   | 400 feet   | 4 miles  | 30 miles |
| Normal | 300 feet   | 3 miles  | 24 miles |
| Slow   | 200 feet   | 2 miles  | 18 miles |

The party can travel a max of 8 hours per day without Forced March.

## Forced March

For each additional hour of travel the party makes, each character must make a Constitution saving throw of DC10 + 1 for each hour past 8 hours.

## Difficult Terrain

Difficult terrain such as forests, deep swamps, ruins, mountains, ice-covered ground, etc. cost double movement so travel is reduced to half the normal amount.
