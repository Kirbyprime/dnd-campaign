## Sources and Caveats

- Mount, vehicle and ship prices and speeds: 2024 Player's Handbook.
- Rentals, charters, passage fares, spell service prices, broom and carpet speeds: Kassoon's 2024 Travel Options page (community compiled).
- Magic item values: 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide rarity values (Uncommon 400, Rare 4,000, Very Rare 40,000 gp).
- Air mount prices are not in the 2024 books. They use a community breeder price list (young animal plus training). Griffon speed checked against the 2024 stat block; Hippogriff, Pegasus and Giant Eagle speeds are from 2014 and not rechecked.
