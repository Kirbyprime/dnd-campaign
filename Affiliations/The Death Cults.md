The Death Cults as they're collectively known are not a unified group at all, but the name given by the various authorities to groups and people who have gone insane in recent years. The cultists scour Faerun, looting, burning, or as their name implies, killing all that they come across. 

Little is known about how cultists are indoctrinated as they tend to kill almost all they come across only staying their hand when it comes to sellswords whom they often hire as protectors. Those captured and tortured simply ramble on about incoherent whispers and voices.

Cultists have rarely been seen fighting one another and seem to intuitively know when the person they're facing is kin.
