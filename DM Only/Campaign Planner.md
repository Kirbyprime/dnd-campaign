### Campaign Hook

Overall campaign is to open the portal and summon forth the [[Herald of Excess]], Envoy of the Old Ones.

### Six Truths Of Your World

* Supernatural whispers and nightmares plague the Sword Coast, bringing insanity to a sizable part of the populace.
* The Church of Tyr has launched a crusade to rid the lands of anything it deems evil and unjust.
* The Death Cults rampage across the countryside, killing everyone in their wake.
* Two factions of The Hand of Yartar, a thieves and assassins' guild in Yartar erupt in warfare, vying for control of the guild.

### Campaign Fronts

What are the major moving forces in this campaign?

#### [[Church of Tyr]]

**Description:** The church hears of a blasphemous artifact and moves to find it. 
**Doom:**  The gate is fortified. The party must break through as well as defend the gate to summon the Herald.

**Three Grim Portents:**
* The Church learns of the location of the [[Book of Eidon]] and moves to secure it.
* The Church sacrifices Cultist prisoners to open the book to learn the location of the gate.
* The Church learns the location of the gate and sends a preliminary force to secure it.

#### [[The Hand of Yartar]] 

**Description:** The guild is in a state of civil war with the two primary factions in a state of uneasy truce. (A) is the leader of one side and treats the [[Silver Key]] as a lucky charm and rules by fear. (B) is the other leader and is more robinhood like.
**Doom:**  The hags eat the heart of (A) as payment then fulfils their end of the bargain by grafting (A)'s head onto (B)'s body, creating an Ettin but is "leader" to both sides hence the guild leader. The Ettin and his followers (other monstrosities) roam the swamp. A [[Silver Key]] can be seen dangling from a necklace on one of its heads.

**Three Grim Portents:**
* An botched assassination attempt by a third party re-ignite the war. (maybe a townsperson who lost a love one.)
* (A) and a few of their followers has been backed into a corner and preparing to make their last stand.
* (A) is captured but (A)'s side refuses to submit. As a last ditch effort, (B) barters with a Coven of Hags in a nearby swamp to become Guild Leader.

#### [[The Death Cults]]

**Description:** Rumors that a village elder has an unusual scar on their face. Cultists have been roaming the area offering death to the willing and unwilling alike.
**Doom:**  The cult is now controls a castle. The party will need to infiltrate it to secure the [[Elder Sign]].

**Three Grim Portents:**
* A small group of fanatics plan to burn down [[Bōs]], a village where the current wielder of the [[Elder Sign]] resides in.
* The [[Elder Sign]] is transferred to one of the Cultists who proceeds to pillage the countryside and grow their forces.
* Strengthened and emboldened, the fanatics siege an isolated castle, intending to make it their new headquarters.
