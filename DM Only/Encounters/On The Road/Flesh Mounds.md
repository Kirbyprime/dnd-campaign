## Setup
A wreaked caravan lies in the middle of the road. The majority of which was burned or pillaged. Blood can be seen along the ground. It would seem that bodies were dragged to the front of the caravan. The party could hear soft sounds of crying.

At the front of a caravan lies a pile of corpses. If the party approaches within 60f, it starts moving and attacks the party.

## Enemies
* Flesh Mound

## Tactics
The mound stays still until the party is within 60f as it has blindsight.

If a party member is engulfed and pass the DC 14 Perception check, they can find that the crying is from the infant's head that acts as the core.

## Loot
* A teddybear
* Orders telling everyone to head to the village of [[Bōs]].

## Expected Outcomes
* Party kills it.
* Party can also bypass it by spending an additional 3 hours to go around it.
