## Setup
A long line of refugees can be seen stopped at what looks like a checkpoint with wooden palisades blocking the road onward. All of them look severely malnourished. Several soldiers can be seen arguing with a family at the very front. The soldiers are suspicious of a family and orders the wagon to be searched. The "father" is on his knees begging them to let them pass as his "mother" is horribly sick and won't survive much longer. The "son" is holding onto the father's sleeve crying while the "wife" is close by sheltering their "daughter" from the argument.

Other peasants are shouting at the soldiers and are close to rioting. The soldiers at the checkpoint are also very tense as they are outnumbered.

If the party can pass a 3 checks before failing 3 checks. They can persuade the guards to let everyone through. If they fail, they notice that the "daughter" has stabbed one of the scouts and killed them. The peasants charge the checkpoint while the party is forced to fight (A). 

If the party investigates the wagon. They notice that there isn't an old woman. Instead, there are cooking tools and the half eaten remains of a soldier. The wife will see you and scream and cause the rest of the refugees to attack.
