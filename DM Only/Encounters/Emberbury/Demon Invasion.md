## Setup
A demonic portal erupts in the middle of town where the sacrificial altar was erected. The demons, angered at the broken contract pour out to slaughter the villagers. For every wave cleared, 20 villagers will be saved.

## Enemies
Waves
1) 2 Swarm of Larvae (larva with the heads of previous villagers)
2) 3 Imp
3) Gibbering Mouther, 2 Imps (gibbering mouther is a mass of all the girls were sacrificed)
4) 2 Swarm of Larvae, Bearded Devil (one of the elders on the elder council)
5) 3 Imps, Incubus
6) Chain Devil (Chain attacks will instead always thematically chain a villager trying to escape, Infernal Chains work as normal)
7) Balor

## Tactics
All the monsters are mindless and will just rush at the nearest member and attack. The bearded devil will chastise the party for breaking the contract and tell them that all the deaths are on them.

## Expected Outcomes
Party runs away at any time during the encounter. Nothing will chase.
