## Setup
The cultists are directing the guards to search the building. The building in question has been ransacked. Any foodstuff or preserves have long since decomposed back to dirt.

## Enemies
* 2 Cultist Fanatics
* 5 Guards

## Tactics
Although the guards the busy searching the area, they are aware enough to see the party as they approach and will not be caught surprised. The guards were hired by the cultists and will run away if both cultists are defeated.

## Loot
* coins and gems looted from the building worth 59g
* a scroll with orders to find and secure the guiding stone, an artifact rumored to be somewhere in the area.

## Expected Outcomes
Party defeats the foes.
