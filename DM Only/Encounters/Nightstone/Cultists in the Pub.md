## Setup
The cultists are directing the guards to search the building. The building in question has been ransacked. Any foodstuff or preserves have long since decomposed back to dirt. If the party attempts to talk to them, the cultists will start rambling and use a command spell to force them to fight.

## Enemies
* 2 Cultist Fanatics
* 5 Guards

## Tactics
Although the guards the busy searching the area, they are aware enough to see the party as they approach and will not be caught surprised. The guards were hired by the cultists and will run away if both cultists are defeated.

Both cultists will start by casting Spiritual Weapon (Bonus Action to summon or act, 60ft range, +4 to hit, 1d8+2, 20ft movement). One will have used Command "Fight" pre-fight, the other will charge in and use Pact Blade. 

All guards rush into melee.

## Loot
* coins and gems looted from the building worth 59g
* a scroll with orders to find and secure an artifact in the shape of a compass, rumored to be somewhere in the area.

## Expected Outcomes
Party defeats the foes.

## Actual Outcome
Party defeated the guards. Used suggestion to get one cultist to do nothing but follow the other, persuaded the other cultist to leave. Cultist dropped the scroll.
