## Setup
The interior of the barracks has been ransacked and in disarray. The remains of tables, benches and crates are spread throughout the main hall. 

To the west of the entrance lies a doorway that would have led to the stairwell. The tower however has long collapsed so the stairwell is buried in rubble with a small cavity located near the top of the doorway. 

A battered chest (mimic) can be seen in the back of the barracks, partially covered by some rotting linen, planks, and giant cobwebs.

## Enemies
* Mimic
* Phase Spider
* 3 Stirges
%%Mimic, Phase Spider, 3 Stirges (65g, Ancient Compass) in barracks%%
## Tactics
The Mimic remains prone until a player touches it at which point it [[Surprised|surprises]] the party.

The Phase Spider attacks if the webs are disturbed. Either by the party touching the mimic, or if the party chooses to touch the web  in some other way. This also [[Surprised|surprises]] the party.

The Stirges will attack if any other enemy is triggered or if the party chooses to disturb the cavity in the stairwell.

## Loot
* 65g and an [[Antique Compass]] can be found in the cavity of the Mimic.

## Expected Outcomes
Party defeats the foes and loots the [[Antique Compass]].
