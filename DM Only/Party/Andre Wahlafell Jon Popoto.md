## Profile

Nobody knows exactly how Andre Wahlafell Jon Popoto came to be. Not even Andre. He remembers two lives that ended before his current one began--or thinks he does.

The first belonged to Andre, a rogue who survived through a dragon ritual in with his interesting companies. One companion decided to hold a lava hot dragon egg. Another could not hit anything to save his life. He shared many wonderful memories with his twin.

The second belonged to Jon, a Dungeon Master. Jon remembered imaginary worlds, monsters with weaknesses, heroes facing impossible odds, dice determining fate, and people gathered around a table creating adventures. He remembered rules that governed reality and stories that always seemed to be heading toward an ending.

But the memories are fragmented, like dreams that fade whenever he tries to examine them too closely. Sometimes they contradict one another. Sometimes he remembers things he has no way of knowing. He cannot tell whether these are past lives, strange dreams, or something stranger.

For his current life, he was raised as an orphan because he does not know who his parents are. He came from an obscure village where each gnome had multiple names but no last names. The village decided not to adopt last names because it is a belief that everyone has equal standing.

His name, Andre Wahlafell Jon Popoto, has always felt strangely familiar, though he has no idea why. Pieces of it seem to echo through the fragments of his dreams--Andre from the rogue's memories, Jon from the Dungeon Master's--while the rest remains an inexplicable mystery, as though someone stitched together a name from lives he was never supposed to remember.
Then there is his magic. It refuses to behave as expected. Spells twist, improbable things happen, and reality seems to laugh along with him.
When the whispers began, he recalled a familiar dream. They seemed to promise a grand adventure filled with discovery, danger, and an ending worth witnessing. Last but not least, he was promised he would finally meet his parents.

Sometimes he suspects that the ending has already been written. He has no proof that his memories are real, that the whispers are connected to them, or that any of it means anything at all.
