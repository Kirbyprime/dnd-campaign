##### Willing Characters

###### Requirement
- The creature must be conscious and willing.
- The creature must be at least one size smaller than the thrower.

###### Operation
- The thrower replaces one of their attacks with a throw. No grapple is required. 
- The range of the throw is equal to 20f times the difference in size between the thrower and the thrown character.
	- e.g. A Medium sized thrower can throw a Small character 20 feet
- The throw passes over creatures, half cover, and three-quarters cover, but **not** total cover.
- This is forced movement: it provokes no opportunity attacks and doesn’t use the target’s movement, action, or Reaction.
- The target lands in an unoccupied space of the thrower’s choice within range.

##### Unwilling Characters
Unwilling characters can not be thrown.
