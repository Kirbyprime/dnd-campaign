## Profile  

Bellatrixy came from a noble, but very private wood elf family. Their only hope for their daughter is to carry on the family tradition, take up the longbow, and become the caretaker of the family woods. Bellatrixy though isn't going to let a bunch of backwaters old fogies stomp on her dreams of becoming the greatest musician of her time. She went along with her family's longbow training, but secretly practiced the art of music. Did you know the bagpipe is a very respected instrument in some magical land far far away? Well, every night she brings their sweet sweet notes to their ears until her family couldn't stand it anymore and kicked her out. Obviously they are jealous that she actually have bigger dreams than they do so off she went to bigger pastures.
