## Profile  

Loraxia Thornweaver detests all things unnatural and thus has a hard time conversing with others. She finds comfort in her animal companions and would rather not deal with others more than necessary. 

Prior to joining the [[The Party | Party]], she lived with her animal friends far from civilization. She would worship the trees around her, sometimes praying to the rain, the morning dawn, or even just the chirping birds. These were all things of the natural world for which she felt reverence for. 

However, progress had no plans of leaving her alone, especially as its loggers felled trees and hunters killed animals she called friends. Cursing her weakness, she wallowed in the forest until the [[The Whispers | Whispers]] started talking to her. They promised her a grand vision: a world where everything would be returned to nature, where cities would crumble and animals would be free to roam as they pleased. All Loraxia had to do was surrender herself to the voices.
