## Profile  

A tall, slender, female Aasimar in her early 20s with glowing pupil-less golden eyes, shimmering copper skin, and short fluffy silver hair. Despite her ethereal look, she has a somewhat vacant expression. She is wearing the garb of a clockwork sorcerer, donned mainly in white with golden gears and symbols. She has feathers along her shoulders due to her celestial heritage, but they have not formed into full wings.

As an Aasimar, Thandresa was raised by her human father, and did not remember her celestial mother. With the world falling apart, hard times hit her village and poverty was rampant. Thandresa followed the guidance of the god of renewal and hope, Lathandre, hoping to restore peace and vitality to the dying world, and bring prosperity to her village. However, her connection to her patron deity was severed when she ate hot pot filled with poisonous mushrooms and came down with a terrible magic-borne illness that left her in an enchanted sleep. With no hope in sight, her father abandoned her, and left her to sleep for two years. In her enchanted sleep, she dreamt of a new life in a beautiful, prosperous land with lush greenery and wildlife. She dreamt she had a husband and an infant son, whom she'd named Picanha. For those two years, she truly believed she was given the gift of living in this beautiful world from her patron deity.

When she finally awoke to see the world around her as chaotic as she'd left it before her illness, and she had no family left waiting for her, she had a hard time believing that this was the true reality, instead of the peaceful place she'd dreamt of. When a new voice began to whisper to her, it effortlessly convinced her that it belonged to her patron deity, and this was not reality. It told her that she had to complete a series of tasks in order to awaken and return to the beautiful world she'd left. By now, she has lost track of how much time has passed since awakening, but she has been devout in following the voice that she believes to belong to her god. She occasionally hears other voices - possibly from her true god Lathandre, or maybe they are just signs of worsening mental illness. However, she chooses to follow the whisper that she believes to be her god. Since she believes that her dream is reality and reality is a dream, she does not feel guilt or remorse for anything she does in this world, as long as she accomplishes what the voice tells her. She is convinced that "the end" that the whispers have told her about is the end of her dream, and once she reaches it, she will awaken back into the beautiful world she'd seen, with her family she'd left behind.
