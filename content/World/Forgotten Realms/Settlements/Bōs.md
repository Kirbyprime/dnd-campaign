## Description

Bōs is a small village to the northeast of Waterdeep. It used to be known for its lush greenery and wildlife before plagues and mutated fungi killed off most of the cattle a hundred years back. Villagers at the time were forced to burn down most of the forests in order to drive back the fungi but the damage was done. This event caused widespread famine and poverty. Bōs has only recently begun to recover and grow their cattle herds once more.

Villagers from Bōs has a unique tradition of giving their children two names. A mundane name for daily use as well as a primal name, used in close company and represents their link to the village as a whole. Primal names are very personal and are rarely told to outsiders. Examples of such names include Asado, Brisket, Chuck, and Filé.

## Locations:

* The Boar's Head
	* A large hut with no walls and just several wooden pillars holding up a hay roof. The centerpiece of the establishment is a giant donut shaped slab of iron suspended on top of fire bricks. The entire slab acts as a grill where the grill master can cook an entire side of cow in one go. The grill is surrounded by makeshift tables, benches, and stools. These are often in different styles or just brought over by the villagers from their own homes.
