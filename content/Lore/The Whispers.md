> The Whispers call. The Whispers beckon.

Nonsensical chatter gnaws at the back of the mind of those who are afflicted by the Whispers. Most, if not all, go insane. [[The Party| Some]] believe that they can make sense of the Whispers, that it is guiding them through tasks and tribulations. They are, perhaps, the most insane of them all.
