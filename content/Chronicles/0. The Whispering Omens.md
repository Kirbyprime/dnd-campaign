Much has been lost since the age before the fall. Culture, knowledge, entire civilizations swept away in a sea of ashes. The oceans are dead; they are now nothing more than deserts filled with bone and rot. The trees are dead; twisted visages of screaming faces protrude from their fossilized bark. The cities, towns, and villages are all dead. 

Not much remains of how it all started, only that certain individuals, the Cursed Ones, heard the whispers. The whispers were seductive, chaotic, sane, and insane all in equal measure. Most who heard them went utterly mad, becoming nothing more than rambling fanatics. Others who fared better became convinced that the End Times were approaching and formed Death Cults, eager to spread the gift of death to all.

For six individuals, however, the whispers did something more. Each person heard what they wanted to hear, believed what they wanted to believe, but the results were the same. It implanted ideas of power and grandeur in their minds, convincing them to forgo their previous lives to partake in a grand quest.

And so the individuals gathered, the whispers guiding them together. None of them know where they were going or what will become of them, only that once they succeed, the world will forever be changed.
