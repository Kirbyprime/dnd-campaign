#### The End Times

Much has been lost since the age before the fall. Culture, knowledge, entire civilizations swept away in a sea of ashes. The oceans are dead; they are now nothing more than deserts filled with bone and rot. The trees are dead; twisted visages of screaming faces protrude from their fossilized bark. The cities, towns, and villages are all dead. However, none of this has happened... yet.

#### The Omens

The world is in chaos. To the north, the guilds of Yartar openly fight in the streets. To the south, bands of bandits and cultists roam freely, pillaging unperturbed. To the east, the Church of Tyr declares a crusade, vowing to rid the world of the corruption that plagues it.

Not much remains of how it all started, only that many began to hear the whispers. The whispers were seductive, chaotic, sane, and insane all in equal measure. Most who heard them went utterly mad, becoming nothing more than mounds of rambling flesh. Others who fared better became convinced that something was coming and turned fanatic, eager to spread the gospel of the whispers to all.

#### The Party Gathers

For six individuals, however, the whispers did something more. Each of them heard what they wanted to hear, believed what they wanted to believe, but the results were the same. They implanted ideas of power and grandeur in their minds, convincing them to forgo their previous lives to partake in a grand quest to fulfill their greatest dreams.

And so the individuals gathered, ignoring the pleas of their loved ones and whatever obligations they might once have had. The whispers guided them to join together in [[The Party | a party]] with a singular goal, to bring about what is fated to come. The End Times.
